It is beyond terrifying to erase everything and start from scratch. I was down bad and I made a choice to risk it all and move to New York City. I took only what I could fit in a couple suitcases (and my cat. you can't leave the cat.) I chose to hit the big red button that self-destructs my entire life in the pursuit of an uncertain future. 

## Down Bad

I was living in Colorado with no job, no money and no hope. Two years prior I graduated Colorado State University with a degree in computer science. Unfortunetly a degree doesn't cut it these days and junior software developer jobs are hard to come by. The interviews were not coming and I lost hope. Something was goiung terribly wrong. Either my resume was garbage, my 0 job experience was a turn off, or it my lack of networking connections gave me no leads. I was broke and down bad. 

## Fractal Tech

In the darkness a glimmer of a glimmer of hope appeared. My friend suggested the Fractal Tech engineering bootcamp. A rigorous software engineering ai accelerator. I totally dismissed it. Then I got to thinking. I had nothing to lose. I started visualizing what life could look like in New York. I started strategizing how I could get there. It would be a difficult journey but eventually I decided that something had to change and Fractal was the best option on the table. So I blew up my life and parted from everything I owned. And here I am now in the coworking space of Fractal. The first week down and 11 more to go. It 
has been rough getting here. It has been hard this week and it will get harder. But I feel the winds changing. Things are looking up.
